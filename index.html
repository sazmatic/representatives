Know Your Weingarten Rights

You have the right to union representation during any meeting that could lead to discipline. Management is not required to tell you this — you must invoke it yourself.

Once you invoke it, management must either:

  • Stop the meeting until your steward arrives
  • End the meeting
  • Offer to continue without representation — decline this

Never attend a disciplinary meeting alone. Always ask for your steward.
